Overview

You can use every workout offline. The app has no account creation, login, cloud sync, in-app purchases, or paid subscription mode. When the device is online, the app may request Google AdMob ads after applicable privacy choices.

The app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google Mobile Ads nevertheless processes ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention as described below. The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location. Notification permission is requested only if you enable workout reminders.

App and operator

This policy applies to the Android app Home Training — No Equipment, package identifier com.offlinefit.hometraining, created and operated by Ayon Mallick. Privacy and support contact: ayonmallick123@gmail.com.

The app provides general fitness and exercise guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Stop exercising and seek qualified medical advice if you experience pain, dizziness or other concerning symptoms.

Android permissions and device access

  • Internet and network state: privacy messaging, opening public policy links, and ads when online. Workouts remain available offline.
  • Notifications: requested at runtime only if you enable workout reminders; you can deny or revoke it in Android settings.
  • Advertising ID: declared by Google Mobile Ads for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Android lets you reset or delete this identifier.

The app does not request contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, precise or approximate location, activity-recognition, body-sensor, Bluetooth or storage/media-library permissions.

Google AdMob and advertising

Online users may see an anchored banner on browsing screens, one interstitial only at the natural break after completing a workout, and a frequency-limited app-open ad on a later safe return to the app. App-open ads are not shown on first launch or during workouts. Ads are not placed on loading or empty screens or where they overlap navigation controls, and full-screen ads are not stacked over other ads. A missing or declined ad never blocks a workout.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on region and your choices, Google and its partners may process identifiers and usage data for personalized, non-personalized or limited advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Learn more in Google's partner-sites notice, Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads' Play data disclosure.

EEA, UK and Switzerland

The app asks UMP to update consent information at launch and requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. Where required, UMP displays a Google-certified consent message. If Google indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, the app shows Settings → Ad privacy choices so you can revisit or withdraw choices.

California and other US states

Where configured and applicable, UMP can record Global Privacy Platform choices that the Google Mobile Ads SDK respects. You may use the in-app ad privacy choices entry point when shown or contact us to exercise applicable access, deletion, correction, or opt-out rights. We do not directly sell personal information; advertising-related processing by partners may be treated as “sharing” or targeted advertising under some state laws.

Consent messages, partners and regional behavior must also be configured by the publisher in AdMob's Privacy & messaging console. The app alone cannot replace that account configuration.

Service providers and data sharing

  • Google AdMob processes advertising data as described above.

We share data only to operate these requested features, comply with law, protect users and services, or complete a business transfer with appropriate safeguards. We do not provide workout history to advertisers.

Your choices and rights

  • Use every workout offline; no account is required or available.
  • Decline optional notification permission or disable reminders in Settings.
  • Use Settings → Ad privacy choices when UMP says the option is required.
  • Reset or delete the Android advertising ID in device privacy settings.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall to delete local workout information.
  • Request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable by emailing us.
  • Object to or withdraw consent for consent-based processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority where applicable.

We may need enough information to understand and verify a privacy request. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction.

Depending on where you live, these may include rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, California and other applicable US-state privacy laws, India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework, or similar laws. We will respond in accordance with the law that applies to the verified request.

Deleting local app data

The app does not create user accounts and the operator does not receive your workout history, favorites, custom routines, reminder choices, or appearance settings. These remain on your device.

You can delete all locally stored app data through Android Settings → Apps → Home Training — No Equipment → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. For advertising-data requests, use the available in-app ad privacy choices and Google's privacy controls, or contact us at ayonmallick123@gmail.com.

Security, retention and international processing

The app disables cleartext network traffic and Android backup. Workout information is stored in the app's private local storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Local data is retained as described above. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising data in countries other than yours using their own legal transfer safeguards.

Legal bases where applicable

Advertising-related processing is based on consent where the law requires it and otherwise on applicable legitimate interests or other permitted grounds. You may withdraw consent through the available privacy controls without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience fitness product and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. The publisher should not select a child-directed Play audience or enable child-directed ad treatment without completing the Google Play Families and AdMob requirements. If you believe advertising data relating to a child was processed through the app, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.
